Our story

Coffee with a
shorter journey.

VERSO started with one drum roaster and a stubborn belief that great coffee should feel personal. We buy small lots directly from growers, roast in-house every week, and serve every cup like it is the first one of the day.

No secret blends. No burnt beans. Just honest coffee, thoughtful milk options and a room that is happy to let you stay a while.

Single origin

Traceable lots, roasted to show the farm, not hide it.

Roasted weekly

Small batches so every bag reaches you at its best.

Low waste

Bring a cup, skip the lid, and save on every refill.
